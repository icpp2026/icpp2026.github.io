## Sightseeing

Several attractions in and around Singapore are worth a visit.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- [**Marina Bay Sky-Park**](https://www.marinabaysands.com/attractions/skypark-observation-deck.html){:target="_blank"}

    Observation deck 57 storeys above Marina Bay.

- [**Singapore Flyer**](https://www.singaporeflyer.com/en){:target="_blank"}

    Giant observation wheel overlooking the bay and the city skyline.

- [**Gardens by the Bay**](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg){:target="_blank"}

    Supertree Grove and the conservatories, next to Marina Bay Sands.

- [**Night Safari**](https://www.mandai.com/en/night-safari.html){:target="_blank"}

    The world's first nocturnal wildlife park, in the Mandai reserve.

- [**The Jewel Box, Mount Faber Peak**](https://mountfaberleisure.com/attraction/mount-faber-peak/){:target="_blank"}

    Hilltop dining and the cable car to Sentosa - also the banquet venue.

- [**Villa Halia, Singapore Botanic Gardens**](https://www.thehalia.com/){:target="_blank"}

    Restaurant set in the ginger garden of the UNESCO-listed Botanic Gardens.

</div>
