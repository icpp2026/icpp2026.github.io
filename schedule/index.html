    Conference Program

    Program at a Glance

    ICPP 2026 takes place September 28 - October 1, 2026. Workshops and tutorials are held on Monday, September 28; the main conference runs Tuesday, September 29 through Thursday, October 1.

    Sessions are held at NTU@one-north on levels 3 and 7. Room codes below refer to Auditorium 302 (AUD302), Lecture Theatres 301/701/702 (LT301, LT701, LT702) and Seminar Rooms 703/706 (SR703, SR706); see the floor plans for how to find them.
